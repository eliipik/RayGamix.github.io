# Official Website & Email Domain Statement

**RayGamix.com** is the official website and official email domain of the RayGamix project. It is the primary source for RayGamix downloads, news, documentation, and other official content.

The **@raygamix.com** email domain is used solely for official RayGamix communications. Email forwarding is enabled only so messages sent to **@raygamix.com** addresses can be received in the official inboxes of the RayGamix team. This forwarding exists for convenience and reliability—it is **not** intended to hide user identities, bypass security systems, or facilitate anonymous or deceptive activity.

Because **raygamix.com** uses email forwarding, some automated services may classify it as a "forwarding email domain." This classification **only** describes how email is delivered. It does **not** indicate that the domain is fraudulent, fake, a knockoff, malicious, risky, or associated with malware or viruses.

RayGamix is committed to maintaining a secure and trustworthy service. To the best of our knowledge, **raygamix.com** does not intentionally distribute malware, collect information through deceptive means, or engage in malicious activities.

As with any website or email service on the Internet, no system can guarantee absolute immunity from security incidents. Although the possibility of an unauthorized compromise is considered extremely low, it can never be completely eliminated. We actively work to protect our infrastructure and address any security issues as quickly as possible.

If a third-party service flags **raygamix.com** solely because it is a forwarding email domain, that should not be interpreted as evidence that the domain is a scam, fake, knockoff, malicious, a virus source, risky, or otherwise unsafe. A forwarding email configuration is a legitimate and widely used method for receiving email and, by itself, is not an indicator of abuse.
